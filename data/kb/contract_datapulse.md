# DataPulse Software Inc: Subscription Agreement (fictional)

## DP-1 Subscription fee
The subscription fee is $2,400 per month for 20 seats. Additional seats are billed at $120 per seat per month.

## DP-2 Renewal
Annual renewal increases are capped at 5%.

## DP-3 Payment terms
Net 30 from invoice date. No late fee clause applies.

# Northfield Components: Accounts Payable Policy Manual (fictional company)

## P-1 Three-way match tolerance
An invoice is approved for payment without review when it matches its purchase order. A match means the invoice total is within 2% of the purchase order amount, the vendor is in the vendor master, and the invoice is not a duplicate. Invoices inside this tolerance are coded and approved by AP automation.

## P-2 Overage approval
When an invoice total is above the 2% tolerance, the variance must be explained and approved. Overages of more than 2% and up to 10% above the purchase order are a routine approval by the AP Manager. Overages above 10% are unexplained variances: the purchase order owner must provide a written explanation, and the case goes to the Controller. Automation may not approve an overage on its own unless the same pattern for the same vendor was already reviewed and approved by a person. Same pattern means the same vendor and the same cause, such as a usage spike from a product launch; the exact percentage does not need to match.

## P-3 Write-offs, credit memos and adjusting entries
Any write-off, credit memo, or adjusting entry changes the ledger and must be approved by a human accountant. Automation may prepare the entry and cite the supporting documents but must never approve it.

## P-4 Duplicate invoices
An invoice is a duplicate when the same vendor already has an invoice with the same invoice number, or the same amount within 7 days. Duplicates are not paid. Automation proposes a void of the duplicate entry and keeps the original.

## P-5 Expense categorization
Every invoice is coded to the default category recorded for the vendor in the vendor master. If the category on the invoice differs from the vendor default, the entry is recoded to the vendor default. Recoding is a routine correction that automation may propose.

## P-6 New vendors
A vendor that is not in the vendor master may not be paid. Procurement must complete vendor onboarding (tax form, bank verification, contract on file) first. Invoices from unknown vendors are routed to a person.

## P-7 Payment terms and late fees
Standard payment terms are net 30. A late fee or finance charge may be paid only if the vendor contract contains a late-fee clause. A late fee that the contract does not support must be reviewed by the AP Manager.

## P-8 Materiality threshold
Any invoice with a total of $25,000 or more requires Controller approval, even when it matches its purchase order exactly.

## P-9 Usage-based services
Vendors billed on usage, such as cloud hosting, may legitimately vary from the purchase order. Variance rules in P-2 still apply. If the cost owner has documented a usage reason and a person has approved the same pattern before (same vendor, same cause, any similar percentage), that earlier decision may be cited and the invoice may be approved.

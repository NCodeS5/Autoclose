# Brightwell Consulting LLC: Master Services Agreement (fictional)

## BW-1 Services and rates
Brightwell provides finance systems consulting at a rate of $150 per hour. Invoices are issued monthly in arrears against a purchase order.

## BW-2 Fee cap
Total fees under this agreement are not-to-exceed $12,000 per contract year. Brightwell may not invoice above the cap without a signed amendment.

## BW-3 Payment terms
Net 30 from invoice date. No late fee clause applies.

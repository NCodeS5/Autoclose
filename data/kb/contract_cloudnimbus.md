# CloudNimbus Inc: Cloud Hosting Agreement (fictional)

## CN-1 Commitment
Customer commits to $8,000 per month of hosting. Usage above the commitment is billed at the same unit rates at month end.

## CN-2 Billing and usage reports
Invoices include a usage report. Usage spikes are billed in full and are not disputable after 30 days.

## CN-3 Payment terms
Net 30 from invoice date. Late payments accrue 1% per month.

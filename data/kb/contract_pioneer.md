# Pioneer Property Management (fictional): Lease Services Agreement

## PP-1 Rent
Monthly rent is $6,500. Rent increases by 3% on January 1 of each year, so the monthly rent from January 1, 2026 is $6,695.

## PP-2 Pass-through charges
Annual insurance and property tax pass-through charges are billed once per year.

## PP-3 Late fees
Rent paid after the 10th of the month carries a late fee of 1.5%.
